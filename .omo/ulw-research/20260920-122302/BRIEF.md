# Footprint compatibility audit

Core question: Are our borrowed Ergogen footprints electrically and geometrically faithful to their upstream designs across front/back/reversible modes, and what master list of popular GitHub sources should we maintain?

Approved format: Markdown (user response). Deliver AUDIT.md and MASTER_CATALOGUE.md plus CSV/JSON evidence. Research only; preserve product files and unrelated edits. Prior research: .omo/ulw-research/20260920-114822/SYNTHESIS.md.

Axes:
1. inventory: Local manifest, provenance and patch integrity
2. switches: Local MX Choc Gateron front back reversible copper
3. controllers: Local MCU and display pad mapping and jumpers
4. peripherals: Local diodes LEDs connectors encoders and polarity
5. integration: App catalogue staging and native Ergogen conversion
6. ceoloide: Ceoloide upstream current and pinned source catalogue
7. infused: Infused-kim upstream current and pinned source catalogue
8. ergogen: Official Ergogen footprint contract and builtins
9. ecosystem_a: Popular GitHub Ergogen footprint repository discovery
10. ecosystem_b: Community repositories and niche footprint coverage
11. maker_repos: Keyboard project embedded reusable footprints
12. licenses: Licensing provenance and redistribution boundaries
13. physical: Manufacturer references for pad numbering and mounting
14. skeptic: Attack compatibility and reversible safety assumptions
15. verification: Executable variant pad/net geometry verification design

Lifecycle: 15-member research team, two expansion waves, fresh refinement team. Repository identity and popularity measured at lookup date; stars are discovery signals, not quality proof. No fabrication readiness without physical evidence.
