# Renderer handoff

Changes: footprint inspection exposes custom filled polygon coordinates/stroke widths/anchor, roundrect chamfers, round/oval drill dimensions and offsets, local segments/arcs/vias, and zone/keepout polygon rings. Copper is transformed from board coordinates to the selected footprint's local frame; board-only generators remain inspectable. All inspector helpers stay inside the portable createTools factory. Existing mappingKey/blank-net work preserved.

SVG and Three previews consume the same geometry. Front/back copper is selected by layer; NPTHs never become copper. The SVG masks all drills across overlapping pads and tracks. Three meshes subtract contained holes and share a stencil mask for holes crossing independent custom copper shapes. Curves are tessellated into64 angular segments (round outlines), preserving physical coordinates. Filled custom polygons include their declared edge strokes and KiCad anchor geometry. ModelMesh moved to a dedicated component without changing asset/loading/transform logic. Camera, model transforms, selection and design tokens retained.

Zone and keepout utilities show unfilled boundaries by layer; keepout boundaries are dashed and named. Notice explicitly says fills/clearances are not simulated. No zone filling solver or DRC claim.

Evidence: app red.txt captures failing custom-jumper contour before implementation; engine-red.txt captures missing polygon/drill/local-copper inspection. app-green.txt:9 passing geometry/SVG tests, including actual Three triangulation drill area, custom anchor/stroke extents, mirrored coordinate frames, side filtering, drilled SVG mask, pad selection and keepout outline. engine-green.txt:26 passing inspector/prior boundary tests, including native engine-generated real nice!nano reversible F/B at37degrees. typecheck.txt and lint.txt are empty success outputs from focused checks. No debug instrumentation or temporary files remain.

Explicit limits/diagnostics: custom primitives other than filled gr_poly (including unfilled polygons, gr_curve/gr_line/gr_arc/gr_circle/gr_rect) are not rendered and report unsupported-pad-geometry; supported polygon stroke widths are rendered. Pad shapes outside rect/circle/oval/roundrect/custom are omitted with warnings. Imported board gr_* graphics report unsupported-preview. Selecting one of several footprints omits board tracks/vias with an ownership warning. Zone display is outline-only. Circular and arc geometry is tessellated, not exact analytic CAD. Browser screenshot/3D stencil visual verification belongs to root's forthcoming integrated build/visual-QA pass; this handoff does not claim browser QA complete.
